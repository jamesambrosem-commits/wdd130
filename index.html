Uganda

Uganda, often called the Pearl of Africa, is a beautiful country located in East Africa. It is known for its rich culture, friendly people, diverse wildlife, and stunning natural landscapes. Uganda is home to Lake Victoria, the source of the Nile River, as well as mountains, forests, and national parks that attract visitors from around the world. Agriculture plays an important role in the country's economy, and Uganda is famous for producing coffee, one of its major exports.
